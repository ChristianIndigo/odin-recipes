Easy Pork Belly Fried Rice

Pick any protein to toss into the mix. Personally, I'm a huge fan of Trader Joe's frozen red Argentinian shrimp.

Ingredients

  • Gyoza
  • Mizo Soup Broth
  • Bok Choy
  • Sliced Shitake Mushrooms

Steps

  1. Cook the mushrooms and bok choy on pan until wilted
  2. Add Miso Broth and Bring to boil
  3. Add Gyoza and simmer for 6 mins
