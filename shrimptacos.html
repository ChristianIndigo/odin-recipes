Simple Shrimp Tacos

Easy to make, healthy, shrimp tacos using only trader joes ingredients.

Ingredients

  • Uncooked Argentinian Red Shrimp
  • Tortillas
  • Coleslaw
  • Pineapple Salsa

Steps

  1. Pan-fry the shrimp until they're thoroughly cooked.
  2. Fill several tortillas with shrimp and coleslaw to make yummy tacos.
  3. Douse each taco with Trader Joe's Pineapple Salsa for sweet-yet-spicy goodness.
